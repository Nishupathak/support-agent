# Store Policies

## Returns
- Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in original packaging.
- Sale and clearance items are final sale and cannot be returned, but defective sale items can be exchanged within 14 days.
- Opened software, gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned.
- To start a return, the customer needs the order number and the email used at checkout.
- Return shipping is free for defective or wrong items; otherwise the customer pays a flat 6 USD return label fee.

## Refunds
- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received.
- Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the item was defective or wrong.
- Refunds over 200 USD require manual approval by a human support agent.
- Refund requests, disputes and chargebacks are handled by a human support agent.

## Shipping
- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
- Orders over 50 USD qualify for free standard shipping.
- Express shipping costs 14 USD.
- Orders placed before 2 PM local warehouse time ship the same business day.
- We currently ship only within the United States and Canada. International shipping to other countries is not available.
- A tracking number is emailed once the order ships.

## Cancellations and Changes
- Orders can be cancelled only while their status is processing, before they ship.
- Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; the customer can refuse delivery or return it after it arrives.
- Shipping address changes are only possible while the order is processing and are handled by a human support agent.

## Warranty
- Electronics carry a 1 year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Accessories and cables carry a 90 day warranty.
- The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage or normal wear.

## Payments
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay.
- We do not accept cash on delivery or cryptocurrency.
- Payment problems, double charges and billing disputes are handled by a human support agent.

## Support Hours
- Human support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time.
